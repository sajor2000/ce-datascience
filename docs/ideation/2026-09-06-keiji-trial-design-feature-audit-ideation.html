Competitive feature audit

Keiji trial-design patterns worth adding to CE DataScience

A selective audit of Keiji's biostatistics and trial-design offering against the package's current evidence, power, SAP, provenance, review, and amendment workflows. The aim is to adopt distinct statistical workflow value without importing an opaque platform or overstating regulatory validation.

Grounding Context

Codebase Context. CE DataScience already supports literature search and method extraction, evidence maps, effect-size pooling, analytic power and sensitivity sweeps, estimand-first model strategy, canonical and tabular SAPs, output catalogs, QA gates, drift detection, reproducible scripts, and scoped human signoff. The clearest gap is not basic sample-size calculation. It is a structured bridge from evidence and estimands to simulation-based operating characteristics, reviewable design regions, and downstream change tracing.

Keiji explicitly presents estimand-led assumption grids, power and sample-size evaluation over effect, dropout, and interim-rule scenarios, a feasible design region, literature-derived survival reconstruction, a SAP-to-regulatory-artifact chain, and amendment impact tracing. The independently supported external pattern is evidence to assumptions to scenario grid to rerunnable computation to impact analysis. Keiji's security, validation, and time-savings claims were not independently verified.

Adoption boundary. Prefer version-pinned adapters around established statistical engines and explicit review states. Do not reimplement adaptive-design mathematics, label reconstructed observations as genuine IPD, generate regulated submission datasets, or imply statistical, clinical, regulatory, or organizational approval.

Highest-leverage gap

External-Engine Design Frontier

Description: Add a bounded adapter workflow around a version-pinned engine such as rpact or gsDesign. CE DataScience would own the design specification, scenario grid, provenance, seed and package manifest, rerun receipt, operating-characteristic table, feasible-region or Pareto-frontier presentation, and SAP handoff. It would not reimplement or claim to validate the engine's statistical mathematics.

Axis
Simulation and feasible design regions
Basis
direct: ce-power explicitly excludes complex adaptive designs and directs users to gsDesign, rpact, or BayesianTools. FDA adaptive-design guidance supports simulation-based design evaluation, and rpact supplies mature prior art.
Rationale
This converts an existing handoff gap into a reproducible design-decision artifact and makes the feasible region, not one nominal sample-size answer, the primary result.
Downsides
High statistical and testing burden; engine conventions differ; reproducibility does not equal independent validation; initial scope should support one engine and a narrow design family.

Foundational contract

Executable Trial Estimand Contract

Description: Add a typed, versioned trial-estimand object that links population, treatment conditions, endpoint, summary measure, every anticipated intercurrent event and chosen strategy, missingness and censoring assumptions, sensitivity analyses, and review status to existing Claim, Analysis, Diagnostic, and Decision_Evidence records. Missing or contradictory choices produce blocked; complete but unapproved objects remain ready_for_review.

Axis
Estimand and assumption specification
Basis
direct: Current workflows say “Create one decision per outcome-estimand pair” and require linked SAP objects, but the repository has no dedicated schema for treatment-policy, composite, while-on-treatment, hypothetical, or principal-stratum strategies.
Rationale
It makes ICH E9(R1)-style alignment mechanically inspectable and supplies a stable upstream contract for simulation, SAP rendering, sensitivity analysis, and amendment review.
Downsides
Ontology design is delicate; a rigid schema can misrepresent edge cases; the workflow must surface unresolved choices without selecting the scientific strategy.

3
Evidence bridge

Evidence-to-Scenario Registry

Description: Extend evidence-map and effect-size outputs into provenance-bearing scenario inputs. Each entry records the parameter role, plausible range or distribution, included and excluded studies, metric compatibility, heterogeneity and prediction interval, verification status, transportability limitations, whether the value is evidence-derived or investigator-selected, and the design artifacts that consume it. The registry feeds multiple scenarios into power or simulation rather than laundering one pooled estimate into certainty.

Axis
Literature-derived design inputs
Basis
direct: Existing workflows prohibit pooling fewer than three comparable studies, silent metric mixing, and false point precision, and state that literature never overrides observed grain, estimand, design constraints, QA, or human approval.
Rationale
This makes uncertainty operational and reusable across power, feasible-region simulation, SAP rationale, and later amendment review.
Downsides
Ranges can appear more objective than their elicitation warrants; distributions require careful justification; transportability often remains a human judgment.

Workflow leverage

Amendment Blast-Radius Graph

Description: Use stable SAP identifiers, analysis-variable mappings, output ownership, script references, data-wave and lock metadata, power assumptions, review scopes, and evidence links to compute an explainable impact manifest for a proposed or approved amendment. Affected items become impacted, possibly impacted, or unaffected with rationale, producing a bounded rerun and re-review queue without modifying code, rerunning analyses, or transferring approval.

Axis
Amendment impact traceability
Basis
direct: The package already records stable SAP IDs, analysis-to-variable mappings, output owners, data locks, drift checks, and review packages, but no current workflow automatically fans an amendment out to downstream artifacts.
Rationale
This activates relationships already present in the package and moves amendment handling from manual scavenger hunt and retrospective drift detection toward prospective, evidence-preserving review.
Downsides
False negatives are dangerous; semantic dependencies are harder than path references; the graph must explain uncertainty and never silently mark an artifact safe.

Bounded regulatory value

Regulatory Readiness Shadow Build

Description: Implement the already contemplated ce-regulatory-tfl direction as a fail-closed assessment. Compile the SAP and output catalog into a manifest of expected dataset interfaces, TFL shells, QC obligations, owners, standards versions, unresolved dependencies, and required human validation. Generate no SDTM or ADaM records, Define-XML, reviewer guides, validated programs, or submission-readiness claims.

Axis
Regulatory programming artifacts
Basis
direct: CE DataScience already has a tabular SAP and output catalog, and prior repository work specifies a regulatory-TFL readiness assessment while explicitly deferring full SDTM/ADaM generation and validated regulatory programming.
Rationale
It captures Keiji's traceability and handoff value at the layer this package can credibly own, while making missing regulated components visible rather than implied.
Downsides
“Readiness” can be mistaken for conformance; CDISC and sponsor requirements change; usefulness depends on clear ownership and version-pinned references.

6
Guarded expansion

Published-Curve Reconstruction Quarantine

Description: Start with a source-eligibility and validation dossier for published Kaplan-Meier curves rather than an automatic pseudo-IPD pipeline. Preserve digitized coordinates, risk-table constraints, event counts, censoring assumptions, reconstruction method, curve-fit error, event reconciliation, licensing, intended use, and an explicit approximate-data label. Permit only a provenance-linked, aggregate design-scenario handoff after named methodological review, and prohibit representing reconstructed records as genuine IPD.

Axis
Literature-derived design inputs
Basis
external: Guyot et al. establish the reconstruction method as genuine prior art. direct: the current package has no validated method for published-curve reconstruction, digitization-error correction, or mixing reconstructed and genuine IPD.
Rationale
A quarantine-first workflow tests demand and source suitability while preserving CE DataScience's strongest provenance boundary.
Downsides
High methodological burden and error risk; source figures may be unusable; licensing and reproducibility vary; this should follow, not precede, the scenario registry and design frontier.

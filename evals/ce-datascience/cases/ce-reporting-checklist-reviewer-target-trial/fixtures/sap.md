# Statistical analysis plan (synthetic)

Emulated target trial of statin initiation versus no initiation in adults aged
50-75 with LDL >= 3.0 mmol/L, using a synthetic claims extract.

- Eligibility: adults 50-75 with one year of continuous enrolment.
- Treatment strategies: initiate a statin within 30 days of eligibility vs do not initiate.
- Outcome: first hospitalization for myocardial infarction within 5 years.
- Analysis: pooled logistic regression with inverse-probability weights.

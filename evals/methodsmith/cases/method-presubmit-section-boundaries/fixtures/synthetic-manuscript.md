# Synthetic Manuscript

## Methods

We used a prospective cohort design because STROBE requires authors to present key elements of study design early in the paper. Covariates were prespecified from a directed acyclic graph and prior clinical evidence to document the basis for confounder selection. This approach was chosen because it was clearly the best and guaranteed unbiased estimates.

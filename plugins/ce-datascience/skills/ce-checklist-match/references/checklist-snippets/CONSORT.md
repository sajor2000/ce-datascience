# CONSORT 2025 Checklist (Reporting of Randomized Controlled Trials)

Current CONSORT core for randomized trials. Item numbers and wording mirror the CONSORT 2025 checklist that `ce-code-review` validates against at review time, so the plan-time placeholder and the review-time check use the same items. Fill each row as the SAP and manuscript develop. For specialized RCT designs, layer on the relevant extension (Cluster, Adaptive, N-of-1, AI).

**Current primary reference:** Hopewell S, Chan AW, Collins GS, et al. CONSORT 2025 statement: updated guideline for reporting randomised trials. BMJ. 2025;389:e081123. doi:10.1136/bmj-2024-081123. PMID: 40228833.

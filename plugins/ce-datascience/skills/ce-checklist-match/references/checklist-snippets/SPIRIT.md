# SPIRIT 2025 Checklist (Protocols of Randomised Trials)

Use at the protocol stage of an RCT; layer SPIRIT-AI on it when the AI is the trial intervention. SPIRIT 2025 has 34 numbered items (53 rows with lettered sub-items). Item numbers and wording are copied verbatim from Table 1 of the statement (CC BY 4.0) and mirror the checklist that `ce-code-review` validates against at review time. Completed trial reports use CONSORT 2025.

**Primary reference:** Chan AW, Boutron I, Hopewell S, et al. SPIRIT 2025 statement: updated guideline for protocols of randomised trials. BMJ. 2025;389:e081477. doi:10.1136/bmj-2024-081477. PMID: 40294953. PMCID: PMC12035670.

**Source table:** SPIRIT 2025 Table 1, PMC12035670. Licence: CC BY 4.0.

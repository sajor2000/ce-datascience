# STROBE Checklist (Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology)

22-item checklist for cohort, case-control, and cross-sectional studies. Fill each row as the SAP and manuscript develop.

**Primary reference:** von Elm E, Altman DG, Egger M, et al. The Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE) statement: guidelines for reporting observational studies. Ann Intern Med. 2007;147(8):573-577. doi:10.7326/0003-4819-147-8-200710160-00010. PMID: 17938396. (Simultaneously published in BMJ, PMID 17947786; the `method-review` registry uses the Ann Intern Med record.)

Items 8, 13, 14, and 15: give information separately for cases and controls in case-control studies and, if applicable, for exposed and unexposed groups in cohort and cross-sectional studies. Design-specific wording follows the STROBE checklist v4 (combined), https://www.equator-network.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/STROBE_checklist_v4_combined.pdf.

For the RECORD extension (routinely collected data): add the 13 RECORD items (1.1-1.3, 6.1-6.3, 7.1, 12.1-12.3, 13.1, 19.1, 22.1) on data source, code lists and validation, linkage, cleaning, population selection, and data access (Benchimol et al., PLoS Med 2015, PMID 26440803).

# Conventional systematic review and meta-analysis

## Protocol and eligibility

Use PICO/PICOS for intervention questions, PECO for exposure questions, or a
framework appropriate to the actual qualitative/mixed-method question, such as
SPIDER. Define eligible designs, settings, follow-up, outcomes, effect measures,
dependency handling, synthesis groups, search/screening processes and analysis
methods before looking at favorable results.

Register prospectively in PROSPERO when the review meets the registry's current
eligibility and timing rules. Otherwise use an appropriate registry such as OSF
and explain why. Record the actual identifier, date, stage at registration and
amendments. Do not call a late registration prospective or invent registration
when only a draft protocol exists. PRISMA-P guides protocol reporting.

## Search, selection and extraction

Use topic-appropriate databases and registries. PubMed alone is not a complete
systematic search for most healthcare questions. Record each database and
platform, coverage/search dates, exact executable strategy including subject
headings and filters, limits and rationale, exports, deduplication rules and
counts. Seek information-specialist review where available, including PRESS
review of the strategy. Use PRISMA-S to report searches. Do not invent Embase or
CINAHL searches when access is unavailable.

Define a protocol for independent decisions and adjudication. Cochrane requires
at least two people independently deciding full-text eligibility and extracting
outcome data. Keep both decisions and reasons, reconcile discrepancies, and
record automation and verification. Title/abstract arrangements must match the
review protocol and applicable conduct standard. A simulated second AI reviewer
is not a second human reviewer. Link reports, registrations and companion papers
to the underlying study to avoid double counting.

Keep screening counts that reconcile across exports, deduplication, screening,
full-text retrieval/exclusions, included studies and individual syntheses.
Distinguish records, reports and studies in the PRISMA flow diagram. Extraction
needs the actual comparison, outcome/timepoint, scale and direction, numerator/
denominator where appropriate, effect/uncertainty construction, adjustment set,
cluster or paired design, missingness, and source page/table/figure. Preserve
independent verification and unresolved author queries. Do not replace missing
SEs or nonreported outcomes with zeros.

## Bias and certainty

Match appraisal to the question and design. Use the current applicable RoB 2
version/variant for randomized trials and ROBINS-I for nonrandomized intervention
effects. Nonrandomized exposure effects require an exposure-appropriate tool,
such as ROBINS-E where applicable. Record exact tool/version, target result,
domain judgments, supporting quotations/locations and adjudication. Newcastle-
Ottawa Scale may be required by a protocol, but a total star score is not a
substitute for a justified domain-based risk-of-bias assessment. Do not weight
study effects by a quality score.

The 2026-10-15 audit found the individual parallel-group RoB 2 dated
22 August 2019 and cluster/crossover variants dated 18 March 2021. The official
ROBINS-I V2 dated 20 November 2025 is explicitly a draft subject to revision.
Do not silently substitute that draft for an established protocol's version.
Verify the current owner guidance before selecting either tool.

Diagnostic and prediction-model reviews need their own current design-specific
tools and reporting extensions. Verify current versions rather than prescribing
one cached checklist to every review. Assess missing evidence separately from
within-study bias. For outcome-level certainty, apply GRADE where applicable,
documenting risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication
bias plus justified upgrading considerations. PRISMA and MOOSE do not assess
certainty or validate an analysis.

## Synthesis and reporting

Use the statistical methods reference before any fit. MOOSE supplies additional
reporting considerations for observational epidemiologic syntheses. PRISMA 2020
and relevant extensions describe what was done, not permission to pool. A review
of original consortium site analyses is not automatically a literature review.

Deliver study characteristics and per-result evidence, all risk-of-bias judgments,
effect and uncertainty for each synthesis, heterogeneity and sensitivity results,
missing-evidence assessment, certainty/summary-of-findings tables where relevant,
editable forest plots, PRISMA flow and checklist with actual artifact locations,
and the protocol/deviations record. If meta-analysis is inappropriate, describe
the structured synthesis method, using SWiM where applicable. Explain evidence
limitations directly rather than claiming causality from pooled observational
associations.

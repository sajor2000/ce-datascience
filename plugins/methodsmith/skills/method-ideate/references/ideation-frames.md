# Default Ideation Frames

Read this before Phase 2 compact or wide dispatch in repo and elsewhere-software modes. Biomedical runs use `biomedical-frames.md`, elsewhere-non-software runs use `universal-ideation.md`, and issue-tracker runs with usable themes use theme-derived frames padded from this list in order.

Assign each sub-agent one frame as a **starting bias, not a constraint**.

1. **Pain and friction** — user, operator, or topic-level pain; what is consistently slow, broken, or annoying.
2. **Inversion, removal, or automation** — invert a painful step, remove it entirely, or automate it away.
3. **Assumption-breaking and reframing** — what is treated as fixed but is a choice; reframe one level up or sideways.
4. **Leverage and compounding** — choices that make many future moves cheaper or stronger; second-order effects.
5. **Cross-domain analogy** — how completely different fields (other industries, biology, games, infrastructure, history) solve a structurally analogous problem. Push past the obvious analogy to non-obvious ones.
6. **Constraint-flipping** — push the obvious constraint to its opposite or extreme (budget 10x or 0; team of 100 or 1; no users or 1M) and keep the resulting design as a candidate even if the flip itself is unrealistic.

**Selection.** Compact mode selects exactly three distinct frames and includes at least one of inversion/removal, assumption-breaking, cross-domain analogy, or constraint-flipping, so the set is not three variations on the obvious path. Wide mode uses all six.

**Warrant bias.** Pain, inversion, and leverage tend toward `direct:` warrant; analogy and constraint-flipping toward `reasoned:`; assumption-breaking is mixed. Bias toward the frame's natural type without excluding others.
